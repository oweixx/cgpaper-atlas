The collection

Eight venues. One place to explore.

This atlas brings together SIGGRAPH, SIGGRAPH Asia, Eurographics, Pacific Graphics, ACM TOG, Computer Graphics Forum, TVCG, and Computers & Graphics. It combines audited bibliographic records with publisher-deposited metadata.

Where the data comes from

  • DBLP SPARQL: audited conference and journal records, ordered authors, exact venue memberships and proceedings editions.
  • Crossref: complete indexed journal-article inventories for the four approved journal ISSNs; DOI metadata and available abstracts.
  • OpenAlex: exact DOI matches for citations, abstracts, open PDF links, work-time affiliations and model-generated subject keywords.

One paper, multiple venues

A SIGGRAPH paper published in TOG is one publication with two venue memberships. Selecting both venues still counts it once. Venue-specific filters use that venue's year. Combined filters prioritize the first matching conference, then journal, in the displayed venue order. The stacked chart groups each paper once using this order; journal segments therefore exclude papers already assigned to a conference.

All research articles in TOG, CGF, TVCG and Computers & Graphics are included, even when associated with another conference. Other conferences are not collected independently. Pacific Graphics papers published in The Visual Computer in 2005–2006 are included through Pacific Graphics, without adding that entire journal.

Years, tracks and article categories

Both SIGGRAPH Technical Papers tracks are included. Posters, courses, sketches, demonstrations and other non-paper tracks are excluded. Session listings and the one-page TOG 36(4) presentation records are not counted as new full research papers. The misleading SIGGRAPH 2003 Sketches & Applications and SIGGRAPH Asia 2019 Courses containers are explicitly excluded. Editorials, corrections and front matter are filtered. Surveys and reviews remain searchable and can be filtered by their title-based category.

Conference edition identifiers take priority over publication dates; journal publication years remain separate. Inspect the year audit and journal issue mapping. Historical source gaps remain visible. Current-year coverage is partial: SIGGRAPH Asia 2026 is not yet linked in the DBLP event index, so related newly indexed TOG articles may appear only under TOG. SIGGRAPH 2026 TOG 45(4) is linked using its official publication announcement.

Research areas and countries

Read the classification method and limitations, 64-paper AI-reviewed evaluation, classification audit, and keyword source ledger. The evaluation is not independent expert validation or a population accuracy estimate.

Countries mean institutions recorded on each paper, not author nationality. The explorer country filter includes a paper when at least one author has that country in the affiliation snapshot. International collaborations count once within a selection. Unknown affiliation selects papers with no recorded country, including unmatched papers. Filtered CSV files include country codes and names; missing data is not inferred. OpenAlex author IDs are deduplicated within the selected country, period and topic. Cross-country totals overlap. Unknown affiliations remain unknown; headquarters assignments and author disambiguation can affect the results. Coverage is shown for every selection. See the affiliation ledger.

Reproducibility

The source ledger records queries, retrieval times and expected versus retrieved counts. Equal DOI records merge globally; distinct DOI publications remain distinct. A DOI-less record can match only with an exact normalized title, full author list, shared venue and adjacent publication year. Indexed-source completeness is not proof that every historical paper is indexed.
